# Routers, PPPoE, and Hotspot

Use this guide for tenant-facing router and hotspot help.

## Routers

Tenants can manage routers from `/admin/routers`. A router should have a clear name, connection details, and service flags for the features it supports.

If a router appears offline, first check that it has a stable internet connection and that its VPN or remote management connection is active. If the tenant cannot reconnect it from the dashboard, they should contact support.

## PPPoE

PPPoE customers are managed from the customer and network sections. A PPPoE client is considered billable when the client has activity or a footprint during the 30-day billing cycle.

## Hotspot

Hotspot configuration is managed from `/admin/hotspot`. Tenants can set up hotspot plans, captive portal branding, and payment options. Hotspot revenue used for Netily billing is based on paid hotspot sessions recorded during the active billing cycle.
